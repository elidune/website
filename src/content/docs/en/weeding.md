Weeding is a state of the copy, separate from the loan, from loss, and from archiving.

| State | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| On the shelf | Circulates as usual |
| Candidate | Stays on the shelf. Can still be loaned, renewed, or reserved |
| Withdrawn | No further loan, renewal, or hold |

A candidate therefore stays on the shelf. A withdrawn copy no longer circulates. The record remains. A deposited copy cannot be weeded. See [Deposits](/en/docs/deposits/).

![Weeding list, light theme.](screen:weeding "Copies that are candidates for withdrawal.")

## Change the state

On the copy screen, the weeding control saves the new state and an optional reason (200 characters at most). This is not a field of the ordinary copy edit.

Staff who manage copies use this control. Cancelling a withdrawal that is already under way requires a separate right, granted to the librarian and the administrator in the shipped set.

When every copy still present is withdrawn, the record itself is marked withdrawn. A title hold is refused when no circulating copy remains.

## At the desk

A loan, a renewal, or a hold on a withdrawn copy is refused. The message says so plainly.

The public catalogue does not publish the weeding reason.

Withdrawing a copy is a collection decision. Taking it back is possible while the cancellation right is there, but loans refused during that time are not replayed on their own.
