The events module is on by default. One screen serves the public and staff. An account allowed to edit events creates, edits, and deletes. The management address returns to that same screen.

The public catalogue, on the sign-in page, also lists public events next to search.

## Audiences

An event aims at every audience type, or at a list of types. The staff form offers the switch “all audiences” and the list of types.

## Consent

Sending rests on the account’s consent, changed in the profile. A child account follows the legal guardian’s consent. This page does not copy the body of the messages.

The shipped template exists in French and English. The Email templates tab edits those two languages. Sending uses the account’s French or English, otherwise French then English. German and Spanish on the account fall back to French for this message. See [Languages](/en/docs/languages/).

The unsubscribe link in the messages opens a public page. The same token serves the one-click action offered by the mail software.

## Unsubscribe

The public page, opened from the message, withdraws consent. A mere preview of the message by the mail software does not withdraw consent: it has to be confirmed.
